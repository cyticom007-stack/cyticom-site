Refund Policy

Effective date: 27 Feb 2026

This policy applies to consulting, digital services, and prototype engagement payments made to Cyticom.

1. Non-Refundable Cases

Payments are non-refundable once service delivery has started, consultation has been completed, or deliverables have been shared.

2. Eligible Refund Cases

Refund requests may be considered if:

  • Service has not started, and
  • Request is submitted within 48 hours of payment.

3. Partial Refunds

If work has partially started, any approved refund may be adjusted against time and effort already consumed.

4. Processing Timeline

Approved refunds are typically processed within 7–10 business days to the original payment source.

5. Chargebacks

For unresolved billing concerns, please contact us first before initiating payment disputes or chargebacks.

6. Contact for Refunds

Email refund requests with payment proof and details to: cyticom007@gmail.com
